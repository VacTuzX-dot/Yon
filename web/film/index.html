Yon in 90 seconds

A film about Yon, the free open-source app for sending files between Macs, Windows PCs and phones. Someone wonders how to get a 4 GB video onto the PC across the room; a friend texts "try Yon". Then a flight over blocky islands shows how it works: finding nearby devices, a TLS handshake, the file sent as sealed chunks and checked on arrival, a whole folder, pairing a phone with a QR code and the relay. Back in the room, they accept the file on the PC. Go to the Yon site.
